A Mobile Companion for D&D5e on Foundry VTT.

Whether you're a player looking for an optimized interface to access your character sheets, spells, and items on the go, or a game master seeking a way to clear the table in your in-person games using Foundry VTT, the VTT Companion app has got you covered.

What is VTT Companion?

The VTT Companion app was born from a simple need at the gaming table: a character sheet that actually fits on a phone and that can be used with Foundry VTT.

It gives players a clean, lightweight mobile character sheet for D&D5e on Foundry VTT, keeping stats, spells, items, and session actions close at hand on phones and tablets.

It relies on the Companion Bridge module to connect to your game, provide real-time sync, and enable two-way interactions when your setup supports them.

Why Use VTT Companion?

IRL Games

  • Perfect for people playing around a table but still using Foundry VTT.
  • No need to try and fit several laptops around the game table anymore.
  • No risk of forgetting your character sheet at home or losing it.

Online Games

  • Integrated for seamless interactions with Foundry VTT system.
  • Keep your screen dedicated to the game, the map, the artworks, etc., while still having access to your character information.
  • Roll everything needed directly from your mobile device and see the results immediately in the game.
How to use the app?

How does VTT Companion work?

VTT Companion works by combining two pieces of software:

Companion Bridge Module

Free / Premium Versions

The Companion Bridge module is installed on your Foundry VTT server. It acts as a bridge between the server and the mobile app, enabling real-time synchronization.

The module is available in both free and premium versions, offering different levels of functionality and integration with Foundry VTT.
